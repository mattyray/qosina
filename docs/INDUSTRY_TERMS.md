# INDUSTRY_TERMS.md — Medical Device Component Terminology

Source: https://www.qosina.com/industry-terms
Only including terms relevant to Qosina's product catalog and the demo.

## Key Terms

**510(k):** FDA premarket submission to demonstrate a device is substantially equivalent to a legally marketed device. Nearly all medical devices must go through this process.

**Acceptable Quality Levels (AQL):** Statistical sampling method for testing product acceptance or rejection.

**Aseptic:** Operation performed in a sterile environment using precautions to prevent bacterial contamination.

**Autoclave:** Chamber for sterilizing with saturated steam using constant high temperature and pressure.

**Biocompatibility:** A material's lack of interaction with living tissue — not toxic, injurious, or physiologically reactive. Doesn't cause immune response.

**Burst Pressure:** The inside-out differential pressure that causes rupture of a component housing.

**California Proposition 65:** Regulates substances with cancer/reproductive harm risk. Qosina products note Prop 65 compliance status.

**CE Marking:** Mandatory conformity marking for products sold in the European Economic Area. "Conformité Européenne."

**Certificate of Compliance (COC):** Document certifying supplied goods meet required specifications. Qosina provides these with orders.

**Certificate of Origin (COO):** Certifies goods in an export shipment are from a particular country.

**Class (Medical Devices):**
- Class I: Lowest risk. Generally don't require FDA pre-market clearance.
- Class II: Could harm if used improperly. Most go through 510(k) process.
- Class III: Could severely injure or kill. Go through rigorous PMA process.

**Cracking Pressure:** The minimum upstream pressure at which a check valve will open and allow flow. Measured in PSI. (Example: Part 91050 has 0.075 psi cracking pressure.)

**EU MDR:** European Union Medical Device Regulation. Replaced previous Medical Device Directive in May 2021.

**FDA (Food and Drug Administration):** US federal agency responsible for regulating medical devices, drugs, food.

**Gamma Irradiation/Sterilization:** Sterilization method using gamma radiation. Some Qosina products are rated for post-irradiation shelf life. (Example: Part 11195 has 36-month post-irradiation shelf life.)

**HDPE (High-Density Polyethylene):** Common material for stopcock handles and some components. Good chemical resistance.

**ISO 13485:** Quality management standard specifically for medical devices. Qosina is certified.

**ISO 80369:** Series of standards for small-bore connectors for liquids and gases in healthcare. Defines connection types to prevent misconnections between different medical applications:
- **ISO 80369-7:** Intravascular and hypodermic applications (most Qosina luer connectors comply)
- ISO 80369-6: Neuraxial applications
- ISO 80369-3: Enteral applications
- ISO 80369-20: Common test methods

**Lot Number:** Unique identifier assigned to a batch of manufactured products. Critical for traceability. If a quality issue is found, the lot number identifies exactly which products are affected.

**Luer:** A standardized system of small-bore connectors used in medical applications. Two types:
- **Luer Lock:** Threaded connection that locks in place. More secure. Used where disconnection could be dangerous.
- **Luer Slip:** Friction-fit (push-on) connection. Easier to connect/disconnect. Used where convenience matters.
- **Male Luer:** The tapered tip that inserts into a female fitting.
- **Female Luer:** The receiving end with the tapered bore.
- **Spin Lock:** A Luer Lock variant where the locking collar rotates freely for easier connection.

**OD (Outside Diameter) / ID (Inside Diameter):** Critical tubing measurements. Components must match tubing dimensions. Example: "Fits 1/4 inch ID Tubing" means the barbed end inserts into tubing with a 1/4 inch interior opening.

**Polycarbonate (PC):** Transparent, strong thermoplastic. Most common material for stopcocks and clear connectors. Good impact resistance.

**Polypropylene (PP):** Chemical-resistant thermoplastic. Used for connectors and fittings where chemical compatibility matters.

**PVC (Polyvinyl Chloride):** Common tubing material. Flexible, clear, inexpensive. Used for luer connector bodies.

**Shelf Life:** The period during which a product maintains its specified properties. Medical device components have defined shelf lives:
- **Non-irradiation:** Standard shelf life (e.g., 60 months for Part 11195)
- **Post-irradiation:** Shelf life after gamma sterilization (typically shorter, e.g., 36 months)

**Silicone:** Flexible, biocompatible material used for valve seals, tubing, and gaskets. Available in different durometers (hardness).

**Traceability:** Ability to track a product back to its origin through documentation. In medical devices, this means tracking from raw material to finished device. Lot numbers enable traceability.

## Connection Type Reference (for compatibility mapping)

| Connection Type | Description | Mates With |
|----------------|-------------|------------|
| Female Luer Lock | Threaded receiving end | Male Luer Lock, Male Luer with Spin Lock |
| Male Luer Lock | Threaded insertion tip | Female Luer Lock |
| Male Luer with Spin Lock | Insertion tip with rotating collar | Female Luer Lock |
| Male Luer Slip | Friction-fit insertion tip | Female Luer Slip, Female Luer Lock (with adapter) |
| Female Luer Slip | Friction-fit receiving end | Male Luer Slip |
| Barbed | Ridged connector that inserts into tubing | Tubing with matching ID |
| Tubing Port | Straight connector for tubing attachment | Tubing with matching ID/OD |
